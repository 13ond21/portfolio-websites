What is Who Knows Me Best?

A party game where the host creates a quiz about themselves, friends join with a 6-character code, and everyone races to guess the correct answers live. Points are awarded for right answers, and the leaderboard reveals who truly knows you best.

Features

❓

Create quiz

Preset question templates or custom questions with your hidden answers.

🔢

Join by code

Share a 6-character join code. Friends join instantly — no signup.

⏱️

Live questions

Timed questions broadcast to everyone. Lock in your guess before the clock runs out.

🏆

Leaderboard

Final rankings with a shareable results card.

💕

Themed packs

Couples, Best Friends, and Party Edition question packs (Premium).

Free vs Premium

Free: unlimited quizzes, up to 6 players, preset question templates.

Premium (£2.99/mo or £1.99 pack): themed question packs, unlimited players, custom branding.

No ads mid-game — party pacing preserved.
