Who Knows Me Best? Privacy Policy

Data controller: Cloudy NI (sole trader)

ICO data protection registration: ZC222090

Your quizzes and answers stay on your device.

Last updated: August 2026

Overview

Who Knows Me Best? is a group trivia app where you create quizzes about yourself and friends guess your answers. All quizzes, answers, and scores are stored locally on your device in the current version.

Data Stored On Your Device

  • Quizzes: titles, questions, correct answers, and join codes are stored locally.
  • Display name: the name you choose appears in lobbies and leaderboards.
  • Scores and answers: player scores and submitted guesses are stored locally.
  • Preferences: subscription state and display name are stored locally.

Data We Collect

We do not collect or sell your personal data. The only third-party services used are:

  • Google Play Billing — processes the optional Premium subscription (£2.99/month) or themed question pack (£1.99). Google handles all payment information.
  • Google Play Services — standard Android platform services.

Sharing

When you share a join code, anyone with the code can open the quiz if syncing is enabled. Answer data is visible only to lobby players in local mode.

Your Choices

  • Delete any quiz at any time.
  • Clear all app data from your device settings, which permanently removes all locally stored quizzes and answers.
  • Cancel your Premium subscription anytime via Google Play.

Children's Privacy

Who Knows Me Best? is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
