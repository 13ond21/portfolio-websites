Delete what is on your device

You do not have to ask us for anything. Everything Who Knows Me Best? saved is in the app's private storage, and Android removes it in a couple of taps:

  1. Open Android Settings → Apps → Who Knows Me Best?.
  2. Tap Storage, then Clear storage. That erases every entry, preference and cached file in one step.
  3. To remove the app as well, tap Uninstall — or hold the app's icon on your home screen and choose Uninstall.

That covers:

  • Quizzes. Titles, questions, the correct answers and join codes.
  • Your display name, which appears in lobbies and on the leaderboard.
  • Scores and guesses from each round.
  • Preferences, including the local record of your subscription or pack purchase.

Shared data we hold, and how to remove it

Who Knows Me Best? has no sign-in and no account, so there is no profile to delete. Nothing is uploaded unless you switched sharing on.

Syncing is opt-in, and only a quiz you have shared reaches us: its questions, correct answers, join code, and the display names and scores of the players in that lobby. Your email address and phone number are never collected. Clearing your phone does not reach the server copy, so to have a shared quiz removed from our project, email support@cloudyni.com with its join code and we delete it within 30 days.

Ask us to delete it by email

Email support@cloudyni.com with the app name and your Google Play order number if you have it. We will confirm in writing, and the request is completed within 30 days. If your message is about data on the phone, clearing the app's storage yourself is faster and just as final.

What that deletes, and what stays

Deleting your data is permanent — once it is gone we cannot restore it, so keep anything you want to keep first. Two things are not ours to delete:

  • A synced quiz's rows in Firebase, which the other players' devices read. Email us and we remove them.
  • The purchase record Google holds against your Google account.

Who Knows Me Best? shows no advertising.

To reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn personalised advertising off, open Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Deleting data does not cancel a subscription

Premium is sold through Google Play as an auto-renewing monthly subscription, or as a one-off themed question pack. A subscription renews until you cancel it in Google Play, and deleting your data does not cancel it.

What we declare to Google Play

Google Play asks for a Data safety declaration for every app. These are the lines we declare for this build, and they match the sections above:

  • Users can create an account — no. There is no sign-in and no account; sharing is a per-countdown/per-quiz choice, not a profile.
  • Account creation is not required and is not available in this version.
  • Users can request deletion — yes (clearing app storage or uninstalling erases everything at once; this page is the deletion URL, and support can confirm in writing if you need it).
  • Deletion requests are completed within 30 days.

Who Knows Me Best? is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
