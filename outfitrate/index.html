What is OutfitRate?

Upload outfit photos, tag them with curated garment categories, and let the community rate your style. Search any item — "black jeans", "white sneakers", "hoodie" — and instantly see every outfit tagged with that item.

Features

👗

Upload & tag

Curated preset tags (tops, bottoms, shoes, accessories, brands, colors, occasions) — no free text.

⭐

Rate outfits

Swipe the feed or rate 1-5 stars. See average scores and rating counts.

🔍

Search by item

Search "black jeans" or filter by category + color to find the exact look.

🚨

Safe & moderated

Report any outfit with a clear reason. 3+ reports auto-flag for review.

Save outfits

Build a saved collection. Free up to 20, unlimited with Premium.
