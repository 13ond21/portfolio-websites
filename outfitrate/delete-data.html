Delete what is on your device

You do not have to ask us for anything. Everything OutfitRate saved is in the app's private storage, and Android removes it in a couple of taps:

  1. Open Android Settings → Apps → OutfitRate.
  2. Tap Storage, then Clear storage. That erases every entry, preference and cached file in one step.
  3. To remove the app as well, tap Uninstall — or hold the app's icon on your home screen and choose Uninstall.

That covers:

  • Uploaded outfit photos, kept in the app's private storage.
  • Your tags (tops, bottoms, shoes, accessories and so on).
  • Your ratings of other outfits, and the outfits you saved.
  • Moderation reports you filed, held locally for review.
  • Preferences, including your display name and the local record of your subscription.

There is no account and no server copy

OutfitRate has no sign-in and no account, so there is no profile to delete and nothing of yours on our servers. Your photos, tags and ratings are stored in the app's private storage on the phone. We hold no server copy of them in this version, so clearing the app's storage deletes all of it at once.

Ask us to delete it by email

Email support@cloudyni.com with the app name and your Google Play order number if you have it. We will confirm in writing, and the request is completed within 30 days. If your message is about data on the phone, clearing the app's storage yourself is faster and just as final.

What that deletes, and what stays

Deleting your data is permanent — once it is gone we cannot restore it, so keep anything you want to keep first. Two things are not ours to delete:

  • The purchase record Google holds against your Google account.
  • Anything you chose to share with another player, which is theirs to delete as well.

OutfitRate shows no third-party advertising.

To reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn personalised advertising off, open Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Deleting data does not cancel a subscription

Premium is sold through Google Play as an auto-renewing monthly subscription. It renews until you cancel it in Google Play (Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions), and deleting your data or uninstalling the app does not cancel it.

What we declare to Google Play

Google Play asks for a Data safety declaration for every app. These are the lines we declare for this build, and they match the sections above:

  • Users can create an account — no. There is no sign-in, no account and no server-side profile.
  • Account creation is not required and is not available in this version.
  • Users can request deletion — yes (clearing app storage or uninstalling erases everything at once; this page is the deletion URL, and support can confirm in writing if you need it).
  • Deletion requests are completed within 30 days.

OutfitRate is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Community moderation, including the report button on every outfit, stays in place before any publishing.
