Delete your data

Last updated: 23 August 2026 · Contact: support@cloudyni.com

Cloudy NI does not collect or store personal data through the CloudyNI Publisher app or the Cloudy NI Apps Facebook page, so there is no account or data store to close. The instructions below cover the two cases where data might exist.

1. Facebook page data

Data on the Cloudy NI Apps page (such as your comments, likes and messages) is held by Meta, not by us. You can delete any of it directly in Facebook:

  • Remove a comment: hover over it → ⋯ → Delete.
  • Remove a like or reaction: tap it again to unreact.
  • Delete or download everything Facebook holds about you: Facebook Settings → "Your Facebook Information".

2. Our Android apps

Our apps store most data on your device. Uninstalling an app removes it. Each app also has its own data deletion page:

  • FactSwipe
  • Pantry Pals
  • Morse Beacon
  • Pass Gen
  • Bluetooth Mic – Karaoke & PA
  • Daily Affirmation

3. Need help?

If you believe Cloudy NI holds data about you and you want it deleted, email support@cloudyni.com with the subject line "Data deletion request". We respond within 30 days.
