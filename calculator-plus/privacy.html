Privacy Policy

Calculator+ · Effective 26 July 2026 · Version 1.1

This Privacy Policy describes how Calculator+ (“the App,” “we,” “us”) handles information when you use our Android application. Calculator+ is a calculator and local utility app. Core user content is designed to stay on your device.

Summary: We do not operate an account system or a developer cloud for your notes or calculator history. User-generated content and preferences are stored on-device. Optional Google Play purchases are processed by Google under Google’s policies.

1. Information the App handles

Depending on how you use the App, it may process:

  • Calculator state — values and inputs needed for calculations and helpers (tips, units, etc.).
  • User-generated content — text notes or other content you choose to create and save in the App.
  • App preferences and security settings — options you configure for app functionality and access control (for example PIN-protected local features).
  • Device-local technical data — standard Android app storage and encrypted preference storage on your device.

We do not require an account with us, and we do not sell personal information.

2. Where data is stored

All user-generated content, notes, and local files related to App features are stored strictly on-device, using industry-standard local encryption where applicable (including Android platform encryption facilities such as AES-based encrypted preferences). Content is kept in the App’s private storage sandbox.

We do not upload your notes or calculator content to developer-operated servers. Device backup or transfer tools are controlled by your device and Google account settings.

3. How we use information

  • Providing calculator and related utility features;
  • Remembering your preferences;
  • Supporting optional local access-control features you enable;
  • Maintaining app stability and basic functionality.

4. Sharing

We do not share your on-device notes or calculator content with third parties for advertising, analytics profiling, or resale. The paid model does not include third-party ad SDKs.

Google Play: Install or purchase data may be processed by Google under the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Permissions

The App requests only permissions needed for declared features (for example vibration for feedback). Core calculator use does not require continuous internet access.

6. Security

We use on-device protections appropriate for a local utility app, including encrypted storage for sensitive preferences and platform sandboxing. You are responsible for physical device security and who can access your device.

7. Children’s privacy

The App is a general productivity utility and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect children’s information via a developer backend (we do not operate one for app content).

8. Your choices

  • Uninstalling the App removes its local data (subject to Android behaviour).
  • You may clear app storage in Android system settings.
  • You control device backups and Google account sync on your device.

9. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The effective date above will change when we do.

10. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent via the support contact listed on the Calculator+ Google Play store listing for the publisher account that distributes the App.

Calculator+ is a calculator and personal utility application. This page describes data practices only.
