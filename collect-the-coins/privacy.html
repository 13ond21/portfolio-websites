Collect the Coins Privacy Policy

Data controller: Cloudy NI (sole trader)

ICO data protection registration: ZC222090

Your coins, your gems, your device.

Last updated: August 2026

Overview

Collect the Coins is a single-player coin pusher game. Your game progress is stored on your device only. We do not operate cloud servers, we do not create user accounts, and we do not sell or share your personal data. The game is fully playable offline.

Data Stored On Your Device

  • Game progress: cash, gems, current stage, upgrades, owned board themes, and the coins currently on the board are saved locally on your device.
  • Preferences: selected theme, tutorial completion, and purchase state are stored locally.

Data We Collect

We do not collect analytics, crash reports, or any personal data. The only third-party services used are:

  • Google Play Billing — processes the optional one-time Premium purchase (£3.99) and gem pack (£1.99). Google handles all payment information; we never see your payment details.
  • Google AdMob — shows a small banner and optional rewarded ads to free players. AdMob may collect the Advertising ID and standard ad-request data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. You can opt out of personalised ads in your device settings, or remove ads entirely with the one-time Premium purchase.

There are no forced ads. Reward ads are always optional and only play when you tap the button.

Offline Use

The game itself never requires an internet connection. Advertising requires a connection to load, but the game never blocks or interrupts your play without it.

Your Choices

  • Delete the app from your device to remove all locally stored game data.
  • Clear the app's data from your device settings to reset your save.
  • Reset or limit your Advertising ID in Android settings, and opt out of personalised ads via Google's ad settings.

Children's Privacy

Collect the Coins is a casual puzzle game rated PEGI 3. It is suitable for all ages, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
