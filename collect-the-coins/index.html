What is Collect the Coins?

Collect the Coins is a warm, relaxing coin pusher game set in a little workshop. Tap the board to drop coins, the pusher shoves the pile toward the ledge, and every coin that falls off gets gobbled up by Pip the Piggy. Reach the stage goal, earn gems, upgrade your machine, and push through 60 hand-tuned stages.

🚫 Zero forced ads · 📴 Fully offline · 💾 Progress always saved · 🔄 Stages never loop

Free vs Premium

Free: the full game, all 60 stages, every upgrade earnable — with a small banner ad and optional rewarded ads for extra gems or a x2 boost.

Premium (one-time £3.99): removes the banner, makes every boost instant (no ads at all), and unlocks the Pink Piggy theme. No subscription, no tricks.

There are no forced ads, no ad breaks, and no paywall for playing offline.
