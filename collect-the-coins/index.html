What is Collect the Coins?

Collect the Coins is a relaxing coin pusher game. Tap the board to drop coins, watch the pusher shove the pile toward the ledge, and collect every coin that falls off as cash. Reach the stage goal, earn gems, upgrade your machine, and push your way through 60 hand-tuned stages.

🚫 Zero forced ads · 📴 Fully offline · 💾 Progress always saved · 🔄 Stages never loop

Free vs Premium

Free: the full game, all 60 stages, every upgrade earnable — with a small banner ad and optional rewarded ads for extra gems or a x2 boost.

Premium (one-time £3.99): removes the banner, makes every boost instant (no ads at all), and unlocks the Royal theme. No subscription, no tricks.

There are no forced ads, no ad breaks, and no paywall for playing offline.
