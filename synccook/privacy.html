Kitchen Buddy (Android package com.luckytools.synccook)
Region: Northern Ireland
Effective date: February 2025

Kitchen Buddy is designed with privacy in mind. All product and food data stays on your device — no account, no cloud database. Ads and Premium are handled by Google Play services.

1. Data Collection

We process minimal data necessary to provide the service. This includes your Premium subscription status (stored on your device) and, where you choose to allow it, Google Play's advertising identifier used to serve ads.

2. Third Parties

We use Google Play for payments (Premium subscription) and Google AdMob for advertising. No Supabase, no RevenueCat, no backend servers — the product and food databases are bundled in the app and work fully offline.
