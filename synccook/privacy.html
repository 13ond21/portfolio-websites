Kitchen Buddy is designed with privacy in mind. All product and food data stays on your device — no account and no cloud database. Ads and Premium are handled by Google Play services.

1. Data Collection

We process minimal data necessary to provide the service. This includes your Premium subscription status (stored on your device) and, where you choose to allow it, Google Play's advertising identifier used to serve ads.

2. Third Parties

We use Google Play for payments (Premium subscription) and Google AdMob for advertising. No RevenueCat and no backend servers — the product and food databases are bundled in the app and work fully offline.

3. Optional cloud backup

Kitchen Buddy works fully offline and keeps your data on your device. If you switch on the optional, end-to-end encrypted cloud backup, that data is stored with Supabase as our processor. Nothing is uploaded unless you turn the backup on yourself. You can remove it at any time by turning the backup off and deleting the copy, or by following the steps on the data deletion page.

4. Data deletion

Local data is removed by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. Backed-up data is deleted as described above. Requests are handled within 30 days.
