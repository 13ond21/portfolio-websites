Delete what is on your device

You do not have to ask us for anything. Everything Countdown Crew saved is in the app's private storage, and Android removes it in a couple of taps:

  1. Open Android Settings → Apps → Countdown Crew.
  2. Tap Storage, then Clear storage. That erases every entry, preference and cached file in one step.
  3. To remove the app as well, tap Uninstall — or hold the app's icon on your home screen and choose Uninstall.

That covers:

  • Countdowns. Titles, target dates, cover emoji and the member list.
  • Comments. Premium comment threads are stored on the device.
  • Your display name — the name other members see when you join a shared countdown.
  • Preferences. Milestone notifications, and the local record of your subscription or unlock.

Shared data we hold, and how to remove it

Countdown Crew has no sign-in and no account, so there is no profile to delete. Nothing is uploaded unless you switched sharing on.

Sharing is opt-in. Nothing is uploaded unless you create or join a shared countdown, and when you do, only the countdown itself is: its title, target date, cover emoji, the member display names, and the premium comments. Your email address and phone number are never collected. That copy is server-side, so it is the one thing on this page that clearing your phone cannot reach — email support@cloudyni.com and we delete the shared rows within 30 days.

Ask us to delete it by email

Email support@cloudyni.com with the app name and your Google Play order number if you have it. We will confirm in writing, and the request is completed within 30 days. If your message is about data on the phone, clearing the app's storage yourself is faster and just as final.

What that deletes, and what stays

Deleting your data is permanent — once it is gone we cannot restore it, so keep anything you want to keep first. Two things are not ours to delete:

  • A shared countdown's rows in Firebase, which are the copy other members see. Ask us and we delete them — see the next section.
  • The purchase record Google holds, which is not ours to delete.

Countdown Crew shows no advertising.

To reset or delete your advertising ID, or turn personalised advertising off, open Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Deleting data does not cancel a subscription

Premium is sold through Google Play as an auto-renewing monthly subscription, or as a one-off per-countdown unlock. If you took the subscription it renews until you cancel it in Google Play, and deleting your data does not cancel it.

What we declare to Google Play

Google Play asks for a Data safety declaration for every app. These are the lines we declare for this build, and they match the sections above:

  • Users can create an account — no. There is no sign-in and no account; sharing is a per-countdown/per-quiz choice, not a profile.
  • Account creation is not required and is not available in this version.
  • Users can request deletion — yes (clearing app storage or uninstalling erases everything at once; this page is the deletion URL, and support can confirm in writing if you need it).
  • Deletion requests are completed within 30 days.

Countdown Crew is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
