Description of Service

Countdown Crew is a shared group countdown app. You can create countdowns, invite friends via shareable links, join others' countdowns, and receive milestone notifications. Free users get unlimited countdowns, default milestone notifications (1 week, 1 day, day of), and up to 10 members per countdown. Premium unlocks unlimited members, comment threads, custom milestone times, and widgets.
