Countdown Crew Privacy Policy

Data controller: Cloudy NI (sole trader)

ICO data protection registration: ZC222090

Countdowns shared with your crew — private by design.

Last updated: August 2026

Overview

Countdown Crew is a shared group countdown app. Your countdowns, member names, and comments are stored locally on your device in the current version, and optionally synced via Firebase only when you enable sharing features. We do not sell or share your personal data.

Data Stored On Your Device

  • Countdowns: titles, target dates, cover emoji, and member lists are stored locally.
  • Display name: the name you choose appears to other crew members when you join a shared countdown.
  • Comments: premium comment threads are stored locally.
  • Preferences: notification settings and subscription state are stored locally.

Data We Collect

We do not collect or sell your personal data. The only third-party services used are:

  • Google Play Billing — processes the optional Premium subscription (£2.99/month) or one-off unlock (£1.99). Google handles all payment information; we never see your payment details.
  • Google Play Services — standard Android platform services.

Sharing

When you share an invite link, that link contains your countdown's ID code. Anyone with the link can open the countdown in the app. If the app is not installed, they will see the Google Play store page. Your email address and phone number are never collected or shared.

Your Choices

  • Delete any countdown at any time from its detail screen.
  • Clear all app data from your device settings, which permanently removes all locally stored countdowns and preferences.
  • Cancel your Premium subscription anytime via Google Play.

Children's Privacy

Countdown Crew is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
