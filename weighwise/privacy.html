WeighWise Privacy Policy

Your data stays on your device. That's the whole point.

Last updated: August 2026

Overview

WeighWise is a weight-tracking application that stores all of your personal data 100% on your device. We have no servers, no cloud sync, and no account system. Your health data never leaves your phone.

Data We Store On Your Device

  • Weight entries, body measurements, goals, and progress photos are stored locally in your device's private storage.
  • App preferences (units, theme, reminder settings) are stored locally.
  • You can export your data anytime as a CSV file, or clear it all from your device settings.

Data We Collect

We do not collect, sell, or share any personal health data. The only third-party services used are:

  • Google AdMob — displays advertising to free users. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID) and usage data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. You can opt out of personalized ads via your device settings.
  • Google Play Billing — processes premium subscription payments through Google Play. Google handles all payment information; we never see your payment details.

Your Choices

  • You can delete individual entries at any time.
  • You can clear all app data from your device settings, which permanently removes all locally stored information.
  • You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings.
  • You can export or import your data as CSV at any time.

Children's Privacy

WeighWise is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at support@cloudyni.com.
