MemeMic Privacy Policy

Data controller: Cloudy NI (sole trader)

ICO data protection registration: ZC222090

Fun audio stays on your device — we don't hear anything.

Last updated: August 2026

Overview

MemeMic is a voice changer and meme soundboard that processes all audio in real time on your device. Your voice is never recorded, uploaded, or transmitted to any server. We have no server-side audio processing and no cloud recording.

Data Stored On Your Device

  • Recorded clips: When you deliberately tap "Record", a WAV file of the processed output is saved to your device's private app storage. These recordings are yours — we never see them.
  • App preferences: Your selected voice effect, output device, gain, and audio settings are stored locally.
  • Premium subscription status: A local flag indicating whether you've subscribed is cached on-device.
  • Downloaded meme packs: If you download premium meme sounds, they are stored in your device's private cache.

Data We Collect

We do not collect or sell your personal data. The only third-party services used are:

  • Google Play Billing — processes the optional Premium subscription (£2.99/month). Google handles all payment information; we never see your payment details.
  • Google AdMob — displays advertising to free users. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID) and usage data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. You can opt out of personalized ads via your device settings.
  • Content Delivery Network (CDN) — fetches meme pack sound files and manifest JSON. Standard network requests that may log IP addresses briefly for delivery.

Microphone Access

MemeMic requests microphone access solely to apply voice effects in real time. Audio is processed locally and is not stored unless you explicitly press the Record button. Your microphone is only active while you have the live processor toggled on.

Your Choices

  • Delete any recorded clip at any time from the Saved Clips screen.
  • Clear all app data from your device settings, which permanently removes all locally stored recordings and preferences.
  • Reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings.
  • Cancel your Premium subscription anytime via Google Play.

Children's Privacy

MemeMic is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
