What is MemeMic?

MemeMic turns your voice into chipmunk, robot, alien, deep, demon, helium, darth and more — in real time. Add a soundboard of meme sounds and play them mid-conversation for maximum chaos. Record clips, share them, and download fresh meme packs each month.

All audio processing happens on your device. Nothing is uploaded.
