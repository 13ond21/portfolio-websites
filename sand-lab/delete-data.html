Delete what is on your device

You do not have to ask us for anything: everything the app saved on the phone is removed by Android in a couple of taps, straight away.

  1. Open Android Settings → Apps → Sand Lab.
  2. Tap Storage, then Clear storage. That erases every board, save slot, preference and cached clip in one step.
  3. To remove the app as well, tap Uninstall on the same screen. Either step on its own is enough to delete the data.

Inside the app you can also clear the board with CLEAR, and overwrite a save slot by painting a new board over it and saving again. Both are immediate, and neither requires an email to us.

There is no account to delete

Sand Lab has no sign-in, no profile and no server-side record of you. There is nothing for us to close, because nothing was ever opened. If you are looking for an account deletion option, its absence is the honest answer: the app has no accounts.

Ask us to delete it by email

If you would still like a written confirmation, or you want to make a request under the UK GDPR, email support@cloudyni.com with the subject “Sand Lab deletion request”. We reply within 30 days.

What we can confirm is the truth and nothing more: we hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to erase. We will say so in writing, and the steps above are the complete deletion.

What that deletes, and what stays

Removed, immediately and permanently, when you clear the app's storage or uninstall it:

  • every board you have painted and every save slot the app was holding;
  • all settings: grid and brush options, tool selection, materials held in the palette and any theme choice;
  • the app's cached copy of your purchase state. Google Play reports it again the next time you open the app, so the purchase itself is untouched;
  • any temporary unlock left over from a rewarded video, and any cached clip the app had stored.

Not held by us in the first place, and therefore not something we can delete:

  • the record of your purchase, which Google holds against your Google account, and any receipt Google keeps;
  • advertising and device data processed by Google and its mediated partners under their own policies, including your advertising ID, which you can reset or delete in Android settings at any time.

Deletion is permanent: once a board or a save is gone, we cannot restore it, because no copy ever existed anywhere but on your device. If you want to keep something, use the app's clip recording and share it out of the app before you clear the storage.

Deleting data does not remove a purchase

A purchase is a record held by Google, not an account with us, so deleting your data — or uninstalling the app — does not remove it and does not refund it.

Sand Lab has no subscription, so there is nothing to cancel and nothing renews. A one-off purchase simply stays with the Google account that bought it: reinstall the app, sign in with that same account, and Restore purchases will bring it back.

Refunds are handled by Google as the merchant of record, under Google Play Terms of Service. The terms of use explain the purchases in full.

What we declare to Google Play

Google Play asks for a Data safety declaration for every app. These are the lines we declare for this build, and they match the sections above:

  • Users can create an account — no. The app has no sign-in and no server-side profile.
  • Account creation is not required and is not available.
  • Users can request deletion — yes (clearing app storage or uninstalling removes every board, save and setting at once; this page is the deletion URL).
  • Deletion requests are completed within 30 days.
  • Data collected: boards, saves and settings are stored on the device and are not collected by us; device identifiers and ad interaction data may be processed by Google AdMob to serve ads, and purchase information is processed by Google Play to sell the paid products.

The declaration is reviewed whenever the app changes. If the app you have does not match what is written here, tell us at support@cloudyni.com and we will correct it.
