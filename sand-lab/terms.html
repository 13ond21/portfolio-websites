Important limitations of the simulation

Sand Lab is an entertainment app: a powder sandbox and a toy. Please read this section rather than skimming it.

  • It approximates physics, it does not model it exactly. Sand Lab uses a cellular automaton on a fixed grid: materials are simplified (there are no molecules, no temperatures in real units, no conservation laws enforced to scientific tolerance) and the results are a plausible-looking approximation on your screen, not a measurement.
  • Its materials are fiction, not chemistry. "Acid", "lava", "gunpowder" and the rest are game elements with invented behaviour. Their effects here say nothing about how the real substances behave, and must not be used to predict, test or plan anything real.
  • Do not rely on it for engineering, scientific, educational or safety decisions. It is not a simulator, a modelling tool or a reference, and it makes no claim to be accurate.
  • Performance and behaviour vary by device. Large grids, extreme material counts and the effects drawn by the render pipeline depend on the phone or tablet you run the app on, so two devices can look and run differently.
