1. The short version

Do you collect my name, email, phone number or address?No. There is no account, no sign-in and no input field in the app.
Do you collect my location, contacts, photos or files?No. The app requests none of those permissions.
Do you sell my personal data?No. We have never sold personal data, and we do not share it for anyone else to sell.
Where is my artwork stored?On your device, in the app's private storage. It is never uploaded.
Do you need an internet connection?The sandbox does not. The simulation, the saves and the sharing are all local. Ads and purchases do need a connection, because Google serves both. See §4 and §5.
Is there advertising?Yes, and only Google AdMob serves it. Its device identifiers are used to show and measure ads. See §4.
Are there purchases?Yes, one optional one-off unlock, processed entirely by Google. See §5.

3. What is stored on your device, and nothing else

Sand Lab keeps three things, all in the app's own private storage. No special permission is needed to read them — which also means other apps cannot read them — and none of it is transmitted to us or to anyone else. None of it is personal data: no personally identifiable information is gathered, no PII is stored on any server, and none is sold.

WhatWhereContains
Save slots — up to three boards, as .sand binary files The app's private files directory (persistentDataPath) The .sand file holds the grid dimensions, the palette version and one 32-bit value per cell. It contains no personal information — not a name, not an identifier, nothing about you.
Local preferences — your chosen element, brush size, haptics setting, view position, and whether you own PRO The app's local preferences store Settings only, so the app reopens the way you left it.
The rolling clip cache — the last few seconds of gameplay The app's private cache directory (temporaryCachePath) Written only when you tap Share. The system may clear the cache at any time; we never upload it.

Uninstalling the app, or Settings → Apps → Sand Lab → Storage → Clear storage, deletes all of it permanently. There is no copy anywhere else, because there is no server.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Ads are how the free tier is funded. They are served by Google AdMob, a service of Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC.

  • What AdMob may access: a device advertising identifier (the Android Advertising ID), plus general device and connection information such as device model, OS version, language, and coarse location derived from the IP address. This is a device identifier, not your identity: it is not your name, your email, or anything that identifies you as a person, and we never receive it in a form that could.
  • Why: to select ads, cap how often you see them, prevent fraud, and measure whether ads worked.
  • Consent: where the law requires it (the EEA, the UK and Switzerland) you are asked for consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before any personalised advertising. If you decline, ads are non-personalised and the advertising identifier is not used for personalisation. You can change your choice at any time from the privacy options inside the app.
  • Purchasing PRO removes banner advertising entirely.

Google's own notice is at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and its advertising controls at adssettings.google.com. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android's Settings → Privacy → Ads.

We do not receive, store or sell the advertising identifier. AdMob reports to us only in aggregate — impressions, for example, and an estimated revenue figure — which cannot be traced back to an individual.

5. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Sand Lab offers three optional one-off purchases — Unlock Complete Sandbox (com.cloudyni.sand_lab.pro_unlock), and the two expansion packs Volatile (com.cloudyni.sand_lab.pack_volatile) and Cosmic (com.cloudyni.sand_lab.pack_cosmic) — plus a rewarded-video trial of certain locked materials. Each is non-consumable: bought once, owned on the account, restorable.

  • Payment is processed entirely by Google. Your card or payment details go to Google Play, never to the app and never to us. We do not see them, we cannot store them, and we cannot refund them ourselves.
  • What the app receives: a signed purchase confirmation, from which it records a single flag — "PRO owned" — in its own local preferences on your device. It keeps no receipt, no order number and no purchase history.
  • Restoring purchases asks Google for that same flag again. Nothing is stored on a server of ours, because there is no server of ours.
  • Google's handling of payment data is covered by the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

6. Sharing a clip

Tapping Share encodes the last few seconds of your board into an animated GIF and hands it, using Android's standard share sheet, to the app you choose (a messaging app, email, cloud storage, and so on).

  • We are not involved in that transfer, and we never see the file. It travels from your device to the destination you picked.
  • The destination app's own privacy policy applies to whatever it does with the file.
  • Nothing is shared until you tap Share. There is no background upload, no telemetry and no automatic posting anywhere in the app.

7. Analytics and crash reporting

The store build of Sand Lab contains no analytics SDK and no crash-reporting SDK. We do not measure how you play, which elements you paint, or how long you stay on a screen.

If a future version adds crash reporting — for example Google Firebase Crashlytics — this policy will be updated first, the change will be recorded in the "Last updated" line above, and the Data safety declarations on Google Play will change in the same release. Any such tool would receive a stack trace and a device model: not your boards, and not an identity.

8. Children

Sand Lab is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect any information from a child, and we could not: there is no account, no input field and no mechanism in the app for a person of any age to send us information.

9. Your rights, and how to use them

Because we hold no personal information about you, most requests resolve to the same answer: there is nothing for us to find, hand over or delete. That is the design, not a dodge. Nonetheless, if you are in the UK or the EEA you have the right to:

  • Access — ask what personal data we hold. The answer is none, and we will confirm it in writing.
  • Erasure — delete what we hold. You can delete everything the app has ever produced, yourself and immediately: CLEAR empties the board, saving again overwrites a slot, and Settings → Apps → Sand Lab → Storage → Clear storage removes the rest.
  • Rectification, restriction, portability, objection — likewise affected by holding nothing.
  • Withdraw consent — for personalised advertising, at any time, from the privacy options in the app. Withdrawing it turns personalised ads off without affecting the rest of the app.
  • Complain to your supervisory authority. Ours is the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk, registration ZC222090); you may also complain to the authority in your own country.

To make any request, email support@cloudyni.com. We answer within 30 days.

For advertising specifically, Google's ad settings (adssettings.google.com) let you control personalisation across Google's network directly, and Android's own Settings → Privacy → Ads lets you reset or delete your advertising ID.

11. Security

The app stores everything in its own private, sandboxed storage, which other apps on the device cannot read. Nothing is transmitted to us, so there is no data in transit for us to secure. Where a third party is involved — AdMob for ads, Google Play for purchases — the transfer to that service uses TLS. We cannot guarantee the security of a device you have lost or that someone else controls; in that case, Android's own device-encryption and screen-lock protections are what protect your boards.

12. International transfers

We do not transfer personal data anywhere, because we do not receive any. Google's ad and payment services may process data outside your country; that processing, and the safeguards it relies on, are described in Google's own privacy notices, linked in §4 and §5.

13. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes we will:

  1. update the Last updated date and the version number at the top of this page;
  2. record the change in the app's changelog; and
  3. where the change meaningfully affects you, tell you inside the app before it takes effect.

Continuing to use Sand Lab after a change means you accept the updated policy. If you do not, the fix is beautifully simple: uninstall the app, and everything it ever stored is gone.
