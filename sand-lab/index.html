A modern powder sandbox

Built the way the classics should have been

No stutter, no grain limit — the whole board is built around a 60 FPS target, which is what makes the details read.

80 materials

Eighty materials, five groups

Powders, liquids, solids & walls, gases & energy, and life — plus air to erase. Nineteen powders alone, from sand and salt to gunpowder, brimstone and phosphorus.

60 FPS target

A Burst-compiled grid

The simulation is compiled for the device, so sand tumbles down a slope, gas curls off a fire and the neon bloom lands smoothly.

Simulation

Real reactions, not colour swaps

Fire spreads through fuel. Acid corrodes what it touches. Water quenches lava into stone and steam. Gases rise and fade, and heavy liquids sink beneath light ones.

The classics

Plant, ice, spout, well, torch

A plant that drinks water and climbs, ice that freezes a pool solid, a spout that fills a basin for ever, a well that swallows what reaches it, and a torch that never burns out.

Chaos

Fuses, C4 and napalm

A fuse walks cell by cell into a C4 charge, napalm burns through a splash of water, and methane rolls fire across a ceiling.

Feel

Neon palette, bloom & haptics

Every stroke lands with a haptic tick and a bloom that reads as heat. Genuine ASMR physics — headphones on.

Canvas

Four brushes, 256² to 384²

Four brush sizes from a single grain to a fistful, and pinch-to-zoom with drag-to-pan across a canvas that grows to 384 × 384.

Safety

Three save slots, written atomically

Your world survives a crash because a save is written in one atomic step. Five switchable looks, with theme, volume and haptic switches remembered.

Share

Always recording the last 4 seconds

One tap makes a looping GIF of what just happened and hands it to the app you choose. There is nothing to set up and nothing to upload.

Private

No account, no internet needed

The sandbox, the saves and the sharing all work offline. We collect no personal information — and no ad ever covers the board.
