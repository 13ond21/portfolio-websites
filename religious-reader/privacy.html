Religious Reader is built privacy-first. There is no account, no sign-up, and no tracking of your reading. Everything you do in the app — saved verses, highlights, notes, streaks, progress, and settings — is stored only on your device. The app is supported by Google AdMob, which processes limited advertising data as described below, and offers an optional monthly "Remove ads" subscription (purchases are handled entirely by Google Play; we never see or store your payment details).

1. Who we are

Religious Reader is an Android app from a developer in Northern Ireland. This policy explains how the Android application handles information when you use it on Google Play.

We are registered with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) as a data controller — registration number ZC222090. You can contact us at support@cloudyni.com.

2. What data the app handles

The app handles no personal information. It does not require an account, registration, or an email address, and it has no cloud sync or analytics SDK. The only third party involved is Google, via the Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK, for delivering the banner ad that keeps the app free.

3. Data that stays on your device

The following is stored only locally on your device (in the app's private storage) and is never transmitted to us or to any server:

  • Saved verses and bookmarks
  • Highlights and notes you create
  • Reading streaks, badges, and progress (e.g. which chapters you have read)
  • Reading-plan progress and reminder preferences
  • App settings (font size, theme, reminder time)

Because this data lives only on your device, it is automatically deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data. There is nothing for us to store, share, or sell.

4. Advertising

The free version shows a small banner ad served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may process:

  • Advertising identifiers (e.g. the Android Advertising ID)
  • Device and operating-system information
  • IP address and general (non-precise) location
  • Log and ad-interaction signals used to detect invalid activity

For users in the European Economic Area and the UK, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for consent before ads are shown; you can withdraw or change consent at any time through the in-app control or your device settings. Where the app is configured for children (COPPA), Google serves only non-personalised ads. All ad traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Google's handling of advertising data is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

5. How we share information

We do not sell or rent your information. The only third party involved is Google (via the AdMob SDK) for the purpose of delivering advertising, as described in section 4. There is no social login, no friend features, and no analytics.

6. Children

The app contains no age-restricted content and is suitable for all ages. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). In the AdMob console the developer has set the appropriate COPPA treatment; when the app is directed to children, Google serves only non-personalised ads.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Opt out of personalised ads: reset or opt out of the advertising identifier in your device's Google Ads settings, and use your device's "Ad privacy" controls.
  • Withdraw consent (EEA/UK): use the consent / privacy-options control shown by the app (Google UMP) or your device settings.
  • Delete your data: uninstall the app or clear its data — this permanently removes everything, because all app data is local.

8. Data deletion

For Google Play's data deletion requirement, see our dedicated page: Request data deletion. Device deletion is immediate when you clear storage or uninstall. Email requests are normally handled within 30 days.

9. Security

We use reasonable measures appropriate to a local-first app. No method of electronic storage is 100% secure. Protect your device with a lock screen and keep the operating system updated.

10. Retention

  • On device: until you clear data or uninstall.
  • Support email: typically up to 24 months, or longer if required for legal claims.
  • Ads: retained by Google under their policies.

11. International

Google Play and AdMob may process data on infrastructure outside the UK/EEA under Google's terms and safeguards.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above will change. Material changes will be reflected in the Play listing where required.
