Use this page for Google Play's data deletion request URL. Religious Reader is offline-first and requires no account. All of your app data — saved verses, highlights, notes, streaks, and progress — never leaves your phone.

Steps to delete your data

  1. Delete local app data (immediate)
    On Android:
    • Open Settings → Apps → Religious Reader → Storage
    • Tap Clear storage / Clear data
    Or uninstall Religious Reader. This removes all local content for that install.
  2. Email a deletion request
    Email support@cloudyni.com with subject "Religious Reader data deletion request". Include:
    • That you are requesting deletion for Religious Reader
    • The Google account email used for Play (only if relevant to prior contact)
    • Any detail that helps us identify prior correspondence
    We will confirm and delete personal data we hold that we can identify.
  3. Advertising
    AdMob may process device identifiers under Google's policies. Reset or limit your advertising ID in Android settings.

What is deleted

  • On your device (clear data / uninstall): saved verses, highlights, notes, streaks, reading progress, and settings for that install.
  • From the developer (after a valid email request): support emails or other personal data we can reasonably identify as yours.

What may be kept

  • Legal / correspondence: limited email records typically up to 24 months, or longer if required by law.
  • Ad networks: AdMob retention under Google's schedules.
  • Device backups: clear manufacturer / Google One backups if they retained app data.

How long requests take

Device deletion is immediate when you clear storage or uninstall. Email requests are normally handled within 30 days (sooner when possible).

No account system: because there is no cloud login for Religious Reader, "account deletion" means removing local app data and any personal data we hold from support contact.
