Why QR Forge

◈ Six content types

Links, Wi-Fi, contacts (vCard), plain text, email and SMS — all the codes people actually use, pre-filled from quick-start cards.

◈ Style it your way

Foreground and background colours with wheel pickers, gradients, centred logos, and module styles — Standard, Rounded, Dots, Blocks.

◈ Print-sharp export

Save a 2048px PNG direct to Pictures/QR Forge, or export a crisp vector SVG with Premium. Perfect for flyers, menus and labels.

◈ Fully private

Everything happens on your device. No accounts, no analytics, no backend, no camera, no scanning. Uninstall and everything is gone.

How it works

  1. Pick a QR type — Link, Wi-Fi, Contact, Text, Email or SMS.
  2. Enter your details — the form adapts to the type you chose.
  3. Make it yours — colours, gradient, logo and module style (Premium).
  4. Create QR — get a full-size preview with exactly what's encoded, then save, share or export.
