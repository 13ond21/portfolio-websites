There is nothing of yours on our servers. UK Pay Calc works offline, with no account and no cloud service, so the only copy of your figures is the one on your phone — and clearing the app's storage deletes it in a couple of taps.

Delete what is on your device

  1. Open Android Settings → Apps → UK Pay Calc.
  2. Tap Storage, then Clear storage. Every salary, tax code, pension rate and saved calculation is erased in one step.
  3. To remove the app as well, tap Uninstall — or hold the app's icon on your home screen and choose Uninstall.

There is no account to delete

UK Pay Calc has no sign-in, no account and no server-side profile. Nothing is uploaded when you use it, so there is nothing for us to look up, export or delete on your behalf.

Ask us anyway, if you would rather

If you have written to us — for example an email about a bug — we hold that correspondence so we can reply. Email support@cloudyni.com and ask us to delete it; we confirm in writing and complete the request within 30 days.

What that deletes, and what stays

  • Deleted: every figure you entered, your saved calculations and the app's preferences, all held in the app's private storage.
  • Not ours to delete: nothing in a purchase history, because the app takes no payments and has no in-app purchases.
  • Not collected in the first place: analytics, crash reports, advertising identifiers — the app ships none of them.

Deletion is permanent, so copy anything you want to keep before you clear the storage.

What we declare to Google Play

  • Users can create an account — no. There is no sign-in, no account and no server-side profile.
  • Account creation is not required and is not available in this version.
  • Users can request deletion — yes (clearing app storage or uninstalling erases everything at once; this page is the deletion URL, and support can confirm in writing if you need it).
  • Deletion requests are completed within 30 days.
  • Data collected: none — no app activity, no diagnostics and no personal data leave the device.
