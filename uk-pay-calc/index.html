What it does

Every deduction, on its own line

No black box: each figure is shown with the band or rate that produced it.

Income tax

The personal allowance, the basic, higher and additional rates, and your own tax code — including 1257L, BR and D0 codes.

National Insurance

Employee Class 1 contributions worked out per pay period, on the part of your pay above the threshold.

Pension

Percentage or fixed-amount contributions, and whether the scheme takes them from gross pay before tax or from net pay after it.

Student loans

Plans 1, 2, 4 and 5, plus the postgraduate loan, each with its own threshold and rate.

Any pay period

Annual, monthly, four-weekly, fortnightly, weekly or daily — switch the view without re-entering anything.

The whole picture

Take-home per period and per year, your effective tax rate, and a bar showing the share of gross pay you keep.

How it works

Four taps to a real number

  1. Enter your pay — in whatever period you are actually paid.
  2. Set the details — tax code, student loan plan and pension rate.
  3. Read the breakdown — every deduction, with the rate behind it.
  4. Compare — change one figure and watch take-home move. Nothing is uploaded: the calculator runs entirely on the phone.
Estimates, not advice. UK Pay Calc is an informational tool. Tax and NI change, and your own payroll may treat pension or benefits differently, so treat the result as a close estimate rather than a payslip. Always check against official HMRC guidance, and take professional advice for anything that matters financially.
