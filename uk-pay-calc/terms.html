What this app is

UK Pay Calc is a calculator. You give it a gross or net figure and it estimates income tax, National Insurance, pension contributions and student loan repayments for a UK taxpayer, and shows the working. It is provided as-is for general information.

It is not advice

The results are estimates only and are not financial, tax, accounting or legal advice. Tax rates, thresholds and your own payroll arrangements change, and an estimate cannot allow for every personal circumstance. Before you act on a figure, check it against official HMRC guidance or your payslip, and take professional advice for anything that matters financially.

Accuracy and availability

We work to keep the bands and rates current, and we correct errors when we find them. We cannot promise the app is free of mistakes, or that every future tax change is reflected the day it takes effect. The app is provided without warranties of any kind, to the extent the law allows.
