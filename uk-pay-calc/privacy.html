The short version

  • UK Pay Calc works offline. The pay figures you enter are calculated on your phone and are never sent to us.
  • There is no account, no sign-in and no server-side profile.
  • There is no analytics SDK, no crash reporting and no advertising in the app.
  • To remove everything, clear the app's storage or uninstall it — see Delete your data.

What the app stores

The values you type into the calculator — salary, pay period, tax code, pension rate, student loan plan — are kept in the app's private storage on your device so the app can reopen with your last figures. Android isolates that storage to the app. Nothing is uploaded, and we hold no copy of it.

What we never do

  • We do not collect, sell or share personal data.
  • We do not use the microphone, camera, contacts, location or any file outside the app's own storage.
  • We do not profile you, and there is no advertising identifier to reset because the app serves no ads.

Children

UK Pay Calc is a finance tool intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds something that changes any of the above — an optional cloud backup, for example — this page is updated before that version ships, and the change is described in the release notes.
