Daily Affirmation is designed with privacy in mind. Most data stays on your device. We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions and Supabase to sync premium status.

1. Data Collection

We process minimal data necessary to provide the service. This includes subscription status and anonymous analytics to improve the app.

2. Third Parties

We use Google Play for payments, RevenueCat for subscription management, and Supabase for backend services.

3. Analytics & Firebase

The app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to collect anonymous, aggregate usage statistics (app opens, screens viewed, features used) so we can improve the app. The text of affirmations is not logged — analytics events only record anonymous facts such as the category of an affirmation viewed or a mood check-in emoji.

The app also uses Firebase Crashlytics (crash and error diagnostics), Firebase Performance Monitoring (performance metrics), Firebase Remote Config (remote configuration and feature flags) and Firebase Cloud Messaging (push notifications). These services are provided by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. You can limit analytics in your Android advertising settings, and where legally required analytics only runs with your consent.
