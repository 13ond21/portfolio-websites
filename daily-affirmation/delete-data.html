Delete your data

Daily Affirmation (Android package com.dailyaffirmation.app)
Contact: support@cloudyni.com

Daily Affirmation stores your favourites, history, mood check-ins and settings on your device. There is no account and no Daily Affirmation server holding your data.

1. Delete data on your device

Open Android Settings → Apps → Daily Affirmation → Storage → Clear storage (or uninstall the app). This removes favourites, history, mood check-ins, preferences and all local content immediately — no email needed.

2. Optional cloud backup

If you enabled the optional encrypted cloud backup, a copy of your app data may be stored with our processor Supabase. Delete it by removing the backup in the app (Settings → Cloud backup → Delete), or email support@cloudyni.com and we will delete it for you.

3. Support emails

If you have emailed us, we hold only that correspondence. To delete it, email support@cloudyni.com with subject "Daily Affirmation data deletion request". We delete identifiable support mail we hold within 90 days and send one short confirmation reply.

4. Premium subscription

Premium is a Google Play subscription. Uninstalling does not cancel billing — cancel under Google Play → Payments & subscriptions. Purchase records stay with Google.
