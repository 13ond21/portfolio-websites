This page is the data-deletion URL for Google Play. Pantry Pals is local-first: most data stays on your device. An optional account and live sync store a copy with our processor Supabase — and you can delete that copy too.

What data exists where

  • On your phone (app storage) — pantry items, consumption logs, display names, profiles and preferences. We cannot access this remotely; it is removed when you clear app data or uninstall.
  • In the cloud, only if you created an account / enabled live sync — your account (email) and a synced copy of your pantry content, hosted by Supabase (eu-west-2). Removed when you delete your account.
  • With the developer — only support emails you send us (and our replies). There is no other Pantry Pals cloud database.

How to delete

  1. Delete everything on your device: Settings → Apps → Pantry Pals → Clear storage (or uninstall the app). This removes all local data immediately — no email needed.
  2. Delete a specific item or profile: open the item or profile and choose Delete in the app. Changes sync to your kitchen if live sync is on.
  3. Delete your account and cloud copy: open the app → More → Live sync → Delete account and type DELETE to confirm. This removes your account and the Supabase copy of your pantry. Prefer email? Write to support@cloudyni.com with the subject “Pantry Pals account deletion” and we delete it within 90 days with a confirmation reply.
  4. Support emails: email support@cloudyni.com with subject “Pantry Pals data deletion request”. We delete identifiable support mail we hold within 90 days and send one short confirmation reply when done.

For Google Play

Google Play asks for a data-deletion mechanism for apps with accounts. Pantry Pals provides account and data deletion on request (by email) as described above, and this page is the data-deletion URL recorded in Play Console.
