User guide

How it works

Fast to learn. No account wall.

1. Add an item

Owner (you or “Everyone”), use-by date, zone and photo. Scan a barcode or speak it in.

2. Stick a signed QR label on it

Scan to see who owns what — forged stickers fail the signature check.

3. Share the fridge

Invite your kitchen by QR code. Everyone sees the same fridge, items and shop list.

4. Run the cleaning day

Weekly reminders, quiet hours and photo proof — past use-by items are fair game.
