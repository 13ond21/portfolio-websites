Decibel Meter Pro is designed with privacy in mind. Most data stays on your device. Subscriptions are handled by Google Play Billing. An optional, end-to-end encrypted cloud backup is available, stored with Supabase (our processor).

1. Data Collection

We process minimal data necessary to provide the service. This includes your subscription status and, on the free tier, the Android advertising ID used by Google AdMob to serve ads.

2. Third Parties

We use Google Play for payments and subscription management, Google AdMob to serve ads on the free tier, and Supabase as the backend for the optional end-to-end encrypted cloud backup.

3. Advertising (free tier)

The free version of the app shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may process the Android advertising ID and standard ad-request data (such as IP address and device information) under Google's policies. You can reset or limit your advertising ID at any time in Android settings. Subscribing to Premium removes all ads.

4. Data deletion

Local data is removed by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. If you used the optional cloud backup, you can delete your account in the app, or follow the steps on the data deletion page. Requests are handled within 30 days.
