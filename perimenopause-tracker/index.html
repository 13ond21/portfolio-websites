Questions

You might be wondering

Do I need an account to use PeriLog?

No. Core use is fully offline and requires no sign-up. An optional account is only used if you want end-to-end encrypted cloud backup.

Is my health data private?

Yes. Your logs stay in private app storage on your phone. If you turn on cloud backup, data is encrypted on your device before it is uploaded — no one else can read it.

What if I forget my cloud backup password?

Because backups are end-to-end encrypted, a forgotten password cannot be recovered — even by us. This is a deliberate privacy choice, and it's explained in the app before you sign up.

Can I export or delete my data?

Yes. You can export a PDF or JSON copy anytime, clear all logs in Settings, or use our data deletion page for account and cloud-backup deletion (within 30 days).

Is PeriLog medical advice?

No. PeriLog is a personal tracking and information tool, not a medical device, and does not diagnose or treat anything. Always talk to a qualified healthcare professional about your health.
