1. Who we are

PeriLog is an Android app developed in Northern Ireland. This policy explains how the application handles information when you use it from Google Play.

2. What data the app handles

Depending on how you use the app, it may process:

  • Cycle and symptom data — periods, flow intensity, symptoms (hot flushes, headaches, fatigue, etc.), mood, sleep quality, energy levels, intimacy, cervical fluid, and custom symptom notes.
  • Photo journal entries — photos you choose to attach to daily logs, stored in private app storage.
  • App preferences — theme, notification settings, onboarding state, and feature toggles stored locally.
  • Purchase status — Google Play may confirm Premium ownership so the app can remove ads.
  • Advertising identifiers (free tier) — used by Google AdMob to show relevant ads.
  • Support messages — if you email us, we process the content of your message to reply.

3. Data that stays on your device

All health data you enter in PeriLog (symptoms, cycles, moods, photos, notes, preferences) is stored exclusively in private app storage on your device. We do not operate a cloud sync service, user accounts, or remote servers that receive your health data. Uninstalling the app or clearing app data removes all local data for that install.

4. Purchases and Google Play

Optional Premium is a monthly subscription (premium_monthly, £1.99/month) sold through Google Play Billing. Payment details are processed by Google, not by us. We only receive an entitlement status to unlock Premium features. Manage or cancel in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.

5. Advertising

The free version contains ads served via Google AdMob. These include:

  • Banner ads — displayed at the bottom of the home screen and log screens.
  • Interstitial ads — full-screen ads shown occasionally when navigating between sections.

Ad networks may process your advertising ID and standard ad-request data under their policies. Premium subscription removes all ads. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in Android settings at any time. Learn more at Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Children

PeriLog is designed for adults tracking perimenopause and menstrual health. It is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data via support email, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your choices and rights

You can clear app data or uninstall at any time — this removes all locally stored health data. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold (for example support emails). Contact us at support@cloudyni.com.

8. Data deletion

See our dedicated page: Request data deletion. Local data is removed immediately by clearing app storage or uninstalling (no email needed). The app has no accounts and no developer cloud database. If you emailed support and want that correspondence deleted, email us with subject "PeriLog data deletion request". We will delete identifiable support mail we hold within 90 days and send one short confirmation reply when done.

9. Security

Health data stays in private app storage protected by the Android operating system. Network traffic for ads and Play purchases uses HTTPS. No method of storage is perfect — protect your device with a lock screen and be mindful of who has physical access to your phone.

10. Retention

Local data remains until you clear app storage or uninstall. Support emails are retained only as long as needed to handle your request, then deleted on request within 90 days (with a confirmation reply). Ad partners retain data per their own policies.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top will change when we do. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
