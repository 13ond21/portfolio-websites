Privacy Policy

Ball Fall (Android package com.cloudyni.ball_fall)

Ball Fall is designed with privacy in mind. Your gameplay data — balls dropped, coins earned, upgrades, level progress — is stored locally on your device and is never uploaded to us. There is no account and no server that receives your game data. You can play fully offline for free.

1. Data we collect

Ball Fall uses Google services for ads, analytics and crash reporting. The following data may be collected by those services:

  • Advertising ID (via AdMob) — used to show ads on the free tier and measure ad performance. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings.
  • App interactions (via Google Analytics for Firebase) — anonymous usage counts (levels started, balls collected, upgrades bought). Amounts and names of game items are never included — only counts.
  • Crash logs & diagnostics (via Crashlytics) — device/OS info and stack traces when the app crashes, so we can fix bugs. Kept briefly and used only for stability.
  • Purchase history (via Google Play Billing) — if you buy “Remove Ads”, Google Play records the purchase. This is handled by Google; we only receive confirmation that the purchase happened.

We do not collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information. We do not sell your data. We do not use your gameplay data for advertising targeting.

2. Advertising

The free version of Ball Fall shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Ads appear between levels only — never during gameplay, and never at the cost of your progress. In the EEA and UK, a consent prompt is shown before ads and analytics run; analytics only runs with consent where legally required.

Google and its partners may collect and process data such as advertising identifiers, IP address and device information to deliver and measure ads, subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Analytics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase for anonymous, aggregate usage statistics so we can improve the game. Analytics events contain counts only — never coin amounts, level targets, or any content you generate in the game.

4. Offline & local data

All gameplay data is stored locally on your device. The app works fully offline at no cost. There is no account and no cloud storage of your game data.

5. Your choices

  • Uninstall the app to remove all locally stored game data.
  • Reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in Android settings.
  • Buy Remove Ads (one-time) to disable ads in the app.
  • Manage analytics consent via the consent prompt shown on first launch.

6. Data deletion

Because your game data lives only on your device, uninstalling Ball Fall removes it. To request deletion or ask a question, email support@cloudyni.com — requests are handled within 30 days. See our data deletion page for details.

7. Children

Ball Fall is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top reflects any changes.

9. Contact

Questions? Email support@cloudyni.com.
