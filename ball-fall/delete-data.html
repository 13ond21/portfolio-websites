Data Deletion

Ball Fall · Cloudy NI
Last updated: 20 August 2026

Your Ball Fall data is stored locally on your device. We do not operate a server that receives your game data, and there is no account.

How to delete your data

Uninstall the app. Removing Ball Fall from your device deletes all locally stored game data — balls, coins, upgrades and progress.

Need help?

If you need assistance, or want to ask about data deletion, email support@cloudyni.com with the subject line “Ball Fall data deletion request”. We respond to all requests within 30 days.

For advertising IDs, you can reset or opt out at any time in your Android device settings (Ads).
