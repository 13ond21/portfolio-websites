How Ball Fall works

Drop

Tap to drop standard balls, or catch a rare x2 / x3 / x5 ball when it spawns. Rare balls are worth multiples of your upgraded standard value.

Multiply

Funnels multiply any ball that passes through. Levels change the funnel layout, so every stage plays differently.

Earn

The grinder turns balls into banked coins and grows your Coin/s idle income — it keeps earning even when you close the app.
