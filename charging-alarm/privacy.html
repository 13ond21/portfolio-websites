Privacy Policy — Charging Alarm

Data controller: Cloudy NI (sole trader)

ICO data protection registration: ZC222090

Last updated: July 31, 2026

Charging Alarm is a battery monitoring utility. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information you provide, and we do not require any account to use the app.

In short: We collect nothing from you directly. All app preferences and charge history are stored locally on your device.

Advertising

Charging Alarm displays advertising provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process device identifiers, IP address, and usage data in order to deliver and measure ads. Personalized ads may be shown based on Google's advertising ID.

You can limit ad personalization at any time:

  • On your device: Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete advertising ID / Opt out of Ads Personalization
  • Via Google's Ads Settings: adssettings.google.com

In-App Purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment details. Subscription and one-time purchase data is managed through your Google Play account.

Permissions

The app requests the following permissions solely to provide its core functionality:

  • Notifications — to deliver alerts when your battery reaches your target level or is fully charged.
  • Foreground service — to keep monitoring your charge level while your device is plugged in.
  • Internet / Network state — to fetch banner ads from AdMob.

Data Storage

All app settings, preferences, and charge session history are stored exclusively on your device. We do not back them up to any server, and we cannot access them.

Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from children. Advertising on the app may be non-personalized by default in regions where required.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
