Delete My Data — Charging Alarm

Data controller: Cloudy NI (sole trader)

ICO data protection registration: ZC222090

Last updated: August 21, 2026

In short: Charging Alarm stores everything on your device. Uninstalling the app removes all of your data.

What data the app holds

Charging Alarm does not operate accounts and does not store your data on any server. All app preferences, charge-target settings, and charging-session history are stored exclusively in local storage on your own device.

How to delete your data

  • Uninstall the app. This removes all locally stored app data, including settings and charge history.
  • Clear app data on the device (Settings → Apps → Charging Alarm → Storage → Clear data) without uninstalling.

Advertising

Advertising is provided by Google AdMob, which may process device identifiers. You can delete or reset the advertising ID at any time on your device (Settings → Privacy → Ads → Reset advertising ID) or opt out of ad personalisation in Google's Ads Settings at adssettings.google.com.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. Purchase records are managed through your Google Play account and can be deleted there.
