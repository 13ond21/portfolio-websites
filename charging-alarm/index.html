Everything to charge smarter

Built to protect your battery — automatically

Simple, private, and free to start. No sign-up, no pop-ups, no account.

🎯 Charge-target alarm

Set any target from 50–100% (80% is the battery-health sweet spot) and get alerted the moment it's reached.

🚨 Full-charge alert

Never overcharge again — a full-screen alarm tells you exactly when to unplug.

⚡ Pick your target on plug-in

A notification offers 80 / 90 / 100 / Custom quick buttons the moment charging starts.

🧩 Home-screen widget

Live battery %, charging status and time-to-target at a glance — no need to open the app.

🌡️ Low-battery & temperature alerts

Warned if your battery drops below your chosen level, or if it runs hot while charging.

📊 Weekly summary

Each Monday, a recap of how many sessions you capped at your target — and the battery wear you likely saved.

🔕 Quiet by default

Silent mode for overnight charging, and a one-tap Dismiss on the alarm without unplugging.

🔒 Privacy-first

Everything stays on your device. No account, no cloud, no tracking. Charge history never leaves your phone.
