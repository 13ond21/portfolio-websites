FactSwipe is an Android app of educational facts for children and families. This policy explains what happens to information when you use it.

Designed for children

The app is designed for children and families. Ads are requested as child-directed, non-personalised, G-rated adverts only, and no full-screen or rewarded ad formats are used.

What stays on your device

Facts, saved favourites (Premium), daily-challenge progress and app settings are stored only in the app’s private storage on your device. We do not upload them. Clearing the app data or uninstalling removes them.

Advertising (free version)

The free version is supported by a single Google AdMob banner ad shown when you are online. AdMob may collect device and advertising-related data (such as the advertising ID) as described in Google’s Privacy Policy and AdMob help. You can reset the advertising ID in Android settings at any time.

Purchases

Premium is an optional monthly subscription billed through Google Play. We do not receive your payment card details. Google processes payments and confirms subscription status to the app so Premium can be enabled and restored.

What we do not do

  • No account, email address or sign-up is required.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not run our own analytics or tracking.
  • There is no cloud backup of favourites or progress.

Delete your data

There is nothing stored on our servers to delete. To remove all data on a device, clear the app data or uninstall it — see Delete your data. To cancel a subscription, use Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.

Children’s privacy

If you believe a child has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above changes when we do.
