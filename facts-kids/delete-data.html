Data deletion

Delete your data

FactSwipe stores facts, favourites, challenge progress and settings locally on your device. An account is optional and is only used for the encrypted cloud backup feature — the app works fully without one.

1. Delete data on your device

Open Android Settings → Apps → FactSwipe → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app. This removes everything on this device immediately — no email needed.

2. Optional account and cloud backup

If you (or your child) signed in to cloud backup, an account (email and password) and an encrypted backup of favourites and progress are stored by our hosting provider (Supabase, UK/EU). To delete the account and backup, email support@cloudyni.com with subject FactSwipe data deletion request. We delete the account and all backed-up data within 30 days and send one confirmation reply. The backup is end-to-end encrypted, so we cannot read its contents.

3. Purchases

Any purchases are handled by Google Play. Uninstalling does not cancel billing — cancel under Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.

For any other questions, email support@cloudyni.com.
