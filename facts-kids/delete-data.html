Data deletion

Delete your data

FactSwipe stores facts, favourites, challenge progress and settings locally on your device. There is no account and nothing is stored on our servers, so there is no server copy to delete.

1. Delete data on your device

Open Android Settings → Apps → FactSwipe → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app. This removes everything on this device immediately — no email needed.

2. Cancel a Premium subscription

Premium is billed by Google Play. To cancel, open Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions and cancel there. Uninstalling the app does not cancel billing.

For any other questions, email support@cloudyni.com.
